# Sources: the register

Every public page a spec in `docs/spec/` cites, made from the specs'
own Sources sections on 2026-11-20. A spec's Sources section is the
record; this list is for finding them all in one place. When a spec
gains or drops a source, this list is brought along.

What may be a source at all is in `CLEAN-ROOM.md`.

## The MusicBee wiki

Its text is under CC-BY-SA. Nothing of it is copied here: the specs say
what a page establishes in their own words, and quote a few words at
most, in marks. "Version" is the MusicBee version the page says it is
up to date with; "Edited" is when the page was last changed. Both were
read on 2026-11-20.
